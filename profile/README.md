# The Usual Idiots

> **Four minds. One mission. Infinite possibilities.**

Welcome to **The Usual Idiots**—a student-led organization created by four students from **SIES** who came together through our major project and a shared curiosity about technology.

We are a small team with big ideas. We learn by building, experiment with emerging technologies, and turn challenging concepts into practical projects. This organization is a space for us to collaborate, grow our skills, and document our journey as we work toward creating solutions that can make a meaningful difference.

## 🌱 Our Mission

Our mission is to explore new technologies and use them to solve real-world problems. We believe the best way to learn is to create, the best way to improve is to collaborate, and the best way to innovate is to stay curious.

Through our projects, we aim to:

- Build useful and thoughtful technology
- Transform ideas into working solutions
- Learn from experimentation and failure
- Share knowledge and support one another
- Grow as developers, problem-solvers, and teammates

## 🚀 What We Do

We work across different areas of technology depending on the problem we are trying to solve. Our interests include:

- Software development and web technologies
- Artificial intelligence and machine learning
- Automation and intelligent systems
- Open-source tools and collaboration
- Research, prototyping, and experimentation

Our projects are driven by learning and impact. Some may begin as ideas for our major project, while others grow from questions, experiments, or technologies we want to understand better.

## 🛠️ Our Approach

We keep our work collaborative and practical:

1. **Explore** — We identify a problem, ask questions, and research possible approaches.
2. **Build** — We create prototypes and turn our ideas into working software.
3. **Learn** — We test, improve, and learn from both successes and failures.
4. **Share** — We document our work so that our progress and knowledge can help others.

## 👥 Our Team

We are a team of four students from SIES, working together as peers and co-builders. Each member brings different interests, perspectives, and strengths to the group, allowing us to approach problems from multiple angles.

Although this organization began with our major project, it represents more than a single academic requirement. It is our shared space to experiment, create, and build a foundation for future ideas.

## 🌍 Looking Ahead

We want to continue growing beyond our major project by:
